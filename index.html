Featured piece

What We Carry Within

“White on the outside. Deep red and gold within.”

Three sculptural pomegranates emerge from a textured white landscape. Similar at first glance, yet each one different, each carrying something deep and precious inside.

The pomegranate runs all through Rebecca's work: a symbol of abundance, beauty and new beginnings. It turns up as ruby-seeded slices, at the heart of a Hamsa, and holding the Shamash on a Hanukkiah.

A Hamsa's journey

Art that goes where it's needed

This Hamsa, mosaic glass on gold with a pomegranate at its heart, travelled all the way to Kingston. She now hangs in the hospital room of a police constable who was hurt protecting the community, watching over him and his family.

He protected our community. Now may this Hamsa protect him.

About the artist

Meet Rebecca Adam

Rebecca Adam is a local Ontario artist and the hand behind HappyArtForYou. She is also a Registered Aesthetic Nurse.

She creates one-of-a-kind art from clay, resin, wood and mosaic, where unlikely materials become friends. Her newest work is a collection of floral paintings: "a little imagination, a splash of colour, and flowers that never fade." Alongside them come sculptural pomegranates, Judaica for the holidays, wire sculpture and ceramics with a sense of humour.

No two pieces are the same. Every original is made by hand, and new work appears on her Instagram first.

Questions

Buying original art from Rebecca

Are the original artworks for sale?

Yes. Rebecca's original artworks are available for purchase. Message her on Instagram or use the form below to ask about a piece, and she will reply with the details.

What materials does Rebecca work with?

Clay, resin, wood and mosaic, often with touches of gold and found objects such as natural bark, brass cartridges and even dry-cleaning hangers.

Is every piece one-of-a-kind?

Yes. Each piece is made by hand and is completely unique, so once an original is gone, it's gone.

Where is Rebecca based?

Rebecca is a local artist in Ontario, Canada.

Where can I see her newest work?

On Instagram at @happpyart4u, where new pieces appear first. The latest posts are also shown at the bottom of this page.
